Battle at the edge of the galaxy

Fight on floating rocks at the very edge of the galaxy. Beat each boss robot and the black hole will carry you to the next one.

7+Cartoon robot battles. No blood, just sparks.

How to play

Bolt's blaster aims at the boss by itself, so you can focus on moving and dodging.

Arrow keys Move anywhere (W A S D works too)
Drag mouse Look around
Space Hold to fly with the jetpack
Enter Hold to fire (clicking works too)
Shift Quick dash
E Bubble shield
Q Super blast when the ring is full
  • Hide behind big rocks and crystals when the boss fires.
  • Fly over the glowing shockwave rings.
  • Shoot the red missiles before they reach you.
  • Grab power-ups: white cross for health, gold bolt for rapid fire, blue shield for protection.
  • If you stop moving the camera, it slowly turns to keep the boss in view.
  • On a tablet, use the joystick to move and drag the screen to look around.
